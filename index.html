About Me:

I live in rural Prince George, BC, where I work on hands-on projects across machining, fabrication, and construction.

I started my machining career in 2002 in Alberta while earning my Red Seal ticket through Northern Alberta Institute of Technology (NAIT) and I have spent over 25 years in CNC machining, programming, manual machining and precision workflow execution.

I am a lifelong learner with a strong interest in practical problem solving. Recently, I have been expanding into timber framing and structural carpentry, and developing a parametric design system and online configurator as a personal project.

I am currently learning to code in HTML and CSS to support that project. I enjoy the technical side of building tools and workflows.

Some hobbies and contributions I am proud of:

While working at Motion, I collaborated with the millwrights and made them a parts puller to their specs and requirements.

I also used my 3D printing experience to propose a small marketing initiative aimed at generating new business. This included designing 3D printed QR codes that we attached to the filter carts we sold, linking customers directly to company home pages, troubleshooting guides, and instructional videos. The original tags I made were from aluminum.

I also designed and repurposed from scrap cut-offs, to machine aluminum company logo keychains, finished with red anodizing, each embedded with a QR code that directed users to our Services department as an additional promotional touch. I was impressed with the appearance after red anodizing a small 100 batch run and the relatively small final size that really worked.

I drew my inspiration for the "work related" QR codes from a Christmas bulb I designed and sent out to family and friends. It was an assembly of 4 different parts. The back side of the bulb had a seperately printed part that just snapped into a slot. I "borrowed" a YouTube video to test the theory for a QR code-link to video.
